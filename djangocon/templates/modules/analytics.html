The privacy guide (/conduct/privacy_guide/) tells visitors they can control non-essential cookies from a banner on first visit, and GDPR/ePrivacy require that consent before analytics cookies are set. So gtag.js is not in the page at all until the visitor accepts: nothing is requested from Google, and no cookie is written, on a first load or after a refusal. The choice is kept in localStorage rather than a cookie -- it never needs to reach the server, and a cookie asking for consent to cookies is a poor look. {% endcomment %}
