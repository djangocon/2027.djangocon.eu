To volunteer, fill in the <a href="https://forms.gle/sGfXDgnfpVgd4wpL9" target="_blank" class="pages-links">volunteer form</a> and tell us which of the areas above you would like to help with.

We will get back to you to match you with something that fits your interests and availability.

Volunteers are expected to follow the <a href="/conduct/code_of_conduct/" class="pages-links">Code of Conduct</a>, like everyone else taking part in the conference.

If you have questions before signing up, write to <a href="mailto:2027@djangocon.eu" class="pages-links">2027@djangocon.eu</a>.
