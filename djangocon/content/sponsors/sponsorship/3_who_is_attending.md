This is a conference for software developers, DevOps, entrepreneurs, marketeers, sprouting new Djangonauts, recruiters and exciting partners working with Python and Django.

The attendees are a mixed crowd with various professional positions and levels. Approximately half are newcomers, and we estimate a full house of 400 attendees in Innsbruck.

DjangoCons exist world-wide and the community and audience are from all continents (except perhaps Antarctica?).

Previous editions were held in Dublin, Athens, Vigo, Edinburgh, Porto, Copenhagen, Heidelberg, Florence, Budapest, Cardiff, Île des Embiez, Warsaw, Zurich, Amsterdam, Berlin and Prague.
