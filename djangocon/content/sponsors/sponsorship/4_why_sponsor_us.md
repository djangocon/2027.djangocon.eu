Five reasons companies come back to DjangoCon Europe year after year.

### 📣 Marketing

Do you have a product, service or brand that is — or could be — loved by Django and Python developers and forward-thinking entrepreneurs across Europe and beyond? Sponsoring puts you in front of them:

- **Digital visibility** on the website, live stream and YouTube recordings.
- Your brand on **printed material**, from credentials to signage.
- A **booth** in the conference hall, where you meet everyone in person.

### 💼 Recruitment

Software developers are hard to find, and the best ones are rarely looking. A conference is where you meet them anyway — hundreds of Django practitioners in one room, for five days, in a setting where talking shop is the whole point.

### ❤️ Giving Back

DjangoCon Europe is run by volunteers on a not-for-profit basis. Your sponsorship goes straight back into the community:

- **Grants** so people from any country can attend an event that often changes their career.
- **Affordable tickets**, so contributors, speakers and newcomers who could not otherwise come still do.

### 🌍 Diversity

Everyone is welcome here, and everyone should be able to attend and feel at ease while doing so. The Django community puts real effort into <a href="https://www.python.org/community/diversity/" class="pages-links">diversity</a> and into being excellent to one another — it is part of the Django Project's DNA. Sponsoring is how that stays true.

### 🤝 Networking

Stand out, meet potential collaborators and find new partners. Most packages include tickets, so your team attends too — and comes back with the talks, the workshops and the connections.
