This is a guide for the organizers, volunteers, and CARE team of DjangoCon Europe on handling <a href="/conduct/code_of_conduct/" class="pages-links">Code of Conduct</a> (CoC) reports. It aims to help us deal with incidents with care, consistency, and professionalism, and gives the CARE (Code of Conduct Active Response Ensurers) team a shared process.

Conferences run under time pressure and social stress; good process exists precisely so people don't have to improvise good judgment in the moment. However, it's always possible that particular circumstances require a deviation from this guide.

Some parts of this guide discuss very severe incidents, like ongoing violence. These events are not expected at DjangoCon, but we want to be as prepared as reasonably possible for anything from the most common minor incidents to the most severe cases.

This is an internal document for organizers, volunteers, and the CARE team, though a public summary may be extracted from it.
