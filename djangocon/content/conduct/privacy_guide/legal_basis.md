We process personal data based on: your consent (for example, opting into the mailing list, or being photographed); performance of a contract (processing your ticket purchase or your sponsorship agreement); and, in limited cases such as safety incidents, our legitimate interest in running a safe event, balanced against your rights. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties beyond what's described below without your consent.
