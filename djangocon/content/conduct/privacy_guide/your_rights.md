Under GDPR, you may access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data at any time by writing to [conduct@djangocon.eu](mailto:conduct@djangocon.eu). We will respond within one month, as required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the [Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde)](https://www.dsb.gv.at/), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria — [dsb@dsb.gv.at](mailto:dsb@dsb.gv.at) — or with your own country's supervisory authority if you are based elsewhere in the EU.
