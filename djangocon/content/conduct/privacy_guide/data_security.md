We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including restricting access to Code of Conduct records to the CARE team and limiting payment processing to PCI-compliant third-party providers. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify the [Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dsb.gv.at/) within 72 hours as required by GDPR, and notify affected individuals directly where the breach poses a high risk to their rights.
