Childcare at DjangoCon Europe and other tech conferences is an essential factor that helps folks who otherwise may not be able to attend. We want to make sure that parents feel welcome bringing their children to DjangoCon Europe and that the availability of childcare is not a barrier to attending.

Childcare applications open on **4 January 2027**. The application form and the closing date will be published here as soon as applications open.

We look forward to seeing you - and your kids - soon in Innsbruck!
