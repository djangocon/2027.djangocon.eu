Innsbruck's centre is compact — most sights, cafés and both <a href="/information/venue/" class="pages-links">conference venues</a> are within a 20-minute walk of the Golden Roof, and the river, the Old Town and the main station are all close together. When you do need to cover distance (or climb a mountain), buses, trams and cable cars have you covered.

### On foot

Walking is the simplest way to get around. The centre is largely pedestrianised, and the two conference venues are about 15 minutes apart along the River Inn. In February, expect ice in the mornings — bring shoes with grip.

### By bus and tram

A dense city network run by <a href="https://www.ivb.at/en" class="pages-links">IVB</a>, with tickets in the IVB app or at machines at every stop. Bus line **F** connects the airport to the centre in 15–20 minutes.

### By cable car

The <a href="https://www.nordkette.com/en/" class="pages-links">Nordkettenbahnen</a> start right in the city centre, at Congress station, and climb through Hungerburg and Seegrube to Hafelekar at 2,269&nbsp;m — city to summit in around 30 minutes. On the south side, the **Patscherkofelbahn** serves Innsbruck's local ski mountain.

### By bicycle

<a href="https://www.ivb.at/en/passenger/mobility/stadtrad/" class="pages-links">Stadtrad</a> city bikes are available at stations across town year-round, and the riverside paths along the Inn are flat and scenic. Watch for ice on winter mornings.

### By taxi

Ranks at the main station and around the Old Town; a trip across the city typically costs €10–€15. Ride-hailing is limited here — locals just call a taxi.

### By regional train

S-Bahn Tirol services run along the Inn valley to Hall, Wattens (Swarovski Crystal Worlds) and beyond — useful if you are adding a day trip.

### By car

You won't need one. The centre is largely pedestrianised and parking is scarce; if you do drive in, use one of the central garages such as Congress or Markthalle.

### Guided tours

- **The Sightseer** — hop-on, hop-off bus linking the Old Town, Ambras Castle, the Bergisel ski jump and the Alpine Zoo. Included with the <a href="https://www.innsbruck.info/en/sightseeing/innsbruck-card.html" class="pages-links">Innsbruck Card</a>.
- **Old Town walking tours** — daily guided walks from the <a href="https://www.innsbruck.info/en/" class="pages-links">tourist information office</a>, covering the Golden Roof, the Hofburg and St. Anne's Column.
