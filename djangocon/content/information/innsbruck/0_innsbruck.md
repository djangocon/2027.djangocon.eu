Welcome to **Innsbruck**, the capital of the Alps. Squeezed between the Nordkette mountain range and the Patscherkofel, centred on the River Inn and crowned by the famous Golden Roof, the city mixes 800 years of Habsburg history with a young, outdoorsy student vibe. Walk its compact Old Town and you'll pass imperial palaces, pastel-coloured riverside houses, cosy coffee houses and, at the end of almost every street, a mountain.


# Travel to Innsbruck
Innsbruck sits in the Inn valley in western Austria, on one of Europe's main north–south Alpine crossings. You can fly directly to Innsbruck, arrive by train from all directions (including night trains), or drive in on the A12 motorway.


### **Flights to Innsbruck**
The city is served by <a href="https://www.innsbruck-airport.com/en" class="pages-links">Innsbruck Airport (INN)</a>, only 4&nbsp;km from the centre. It offers year-round connections (e.g. Vienna, Frankfurt, London) plus many seasonal winter routes — February is high season for Alpine travel, so there are more direct flights than usual, but book early.

If Innsbruck itself doesn't work for your route, nearby alternatives are:

- **Munich (MUC)** — ~2.5–3h by direct train or coach, usually the best-connected option.
- **Salzburg (SZG)** — ~1h50 by train.
- **Zurich (ZRH)** — ~3h30 by train.
- **Verona (VRN)** — ~2h30 by train over the Brenner Pass.

### **Travelling To/From Innsbruck Airport**
Bus line **F** runs every 15 minutes between the airport and the city centre / main station; the trip takes about 15–20 minutes and a single ticket costs a few euros. A taxi to the centre costs roughly €15–€20 and takes about 10 minutes.

### **Innsbruck By Train**
Innsbruck Hauptbahnhof is a hub of the <a href="https://www.oebb.at/en/" class="pages-links">ÖBB</a> network:

- **Railjet** direct services: Vienna (~4h15), Munich (~1h50), Zurich (~3h30), Bregenz and more.
- **Brenner line**: south to Bolzano, Verona and the Italian network.
- **<a href="https://www.nightjet.com/en/" class="pages-links">Nightjet</a> night trains**: comfortable sleeper connections from cities like Amsterdam, Hamburg and Brussels — wake up in the Alps.

### **Innsbruck By Coach**
<a href="https://www.flixbus.com/" class="pages-links">FlixBus</a> and other long-distance coaches connect Innsbruck with Munich, Vienna, Zurich, Verona and many other European cities, often at very low prices.

### **Innsbruck By Car**
Innsbruck lies on the **A12 Inntal motorway**, linked to Germany via the A93/Kufstein and to Italy via the **A13 Brenner motorway**. Remember:

- Austrian motorways require a <a href="https://www.asfinag.at/toll/vignette/" class="pages-links">vignette</a> (buy the digital one online before you travel).
- The A13 Brenner route carries an additional toll.
- In February, winter tyres are mandatory in wintry road conditions.
