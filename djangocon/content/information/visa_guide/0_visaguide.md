If you plan to attend DjangoCon Europe in **Innsbruck, Austria**, it's essential to understand the visa requirements and application process based on your nationality. Here's a comprehensive guide for both EU and non-EU residents.

---

### **For EU/EEA & Swiss Residents**

&nbsp;

#### Do You Need a Visa?

As an EU/EEA or Swiss citizen, you **do not require a visa** to travel to Austria. You can enter the country freely under EU free-movement rules.

#### Documents to Carry

Even though a visa is not required, ensure you carry:

- A valid passport or national ID card.
- Proof of accommodation (e.g., hotel bookings or host details).
- Your DjangoCon Europe ticket or event confirmation.
- Travel insurance (optional but recommended).

#### Travel Tips

- Austria is in the Schengen Area: there are normally no border checks when arriving from other Schengen countries.
- Check your mobile carrier's roaming policies under the EU regulations to avoid extra charges.

---

### **For Non-EU Residents**

&nbsp;

#### Do You Need a Visa?

Austria is part of the **Schengen Area**. Citizens of many countries can visit visa-free for up to 90 days; others need a **Schengen short-stay visa (type C)**. Check the <a href="https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/travel-stay/entry-and-residence-in-austria/entry-and-visa/" class="pages-links">Austrian Ministry for European and International Affairs</a> page for the most up-to-date requirements for your nationality.

If you already hold a valid Schengen visa or a residence permit from another Schengen country, you can generally use it to travel to Austria — no separate Austrian visa needed.

##### Types of Visas

For DjangoCon Europe, you'll typically need a **short-stay Schengen visa (C)** for conferences or business visits.

---

### How to Apply for a Schengen Visa to Austria

&nbsp;

1. **Determine Where to Apply**

   - Apply at the Austrian embassy or consulate responsible for your place of residence (in some countries, applications are handled by an external provider such as VFS Global).
   - If Austria is not represented in your country, another Schengen state may process the application on Austria's behalf.

2. **Prepare Your Documents**
   Gather the following required documents:

   - **Visa application form**: The Schengen visa application form, completed and signed.
   - **Passport**: Issued within the last 10 years and valid for at least 3 months after your intended departure from the Schengen Area.
   - **Photograph**: Passport-sized photo meeting Schengen specifications.
   - **Proof of purpose**: DjangoCon Europe invitation or ticket confirmation.
   - **Accommodation details**: Hotel booking or a letter from your host.
   - **Travel itinerary**: Round-trip flight bookings or travel reservations.
   - **Financial proof**: Bank statements showing sufficient funds or grant approval from DjangoCon Europe.
   - **Travel medical insurance**: Minimum coverage of €30,000, valid throughout the Schengen Area — mandatory for Schengen visas.

3. **Submit Your Application**

   - Book an appointment and submit your documents in person (biometrics are collected on first application).
   - Pay the visa fee (currently €90 for adults, with reductions and waivers for some categories).
   - Apply no earlier than 6 months and no later than 15 days before your trip.

4. **Wait for a Decision**
   - Processing normally takes **up to 15 calendar days**, but can extend to 45 days in some cases — start early, especially since February is peak travel season in the Alps.

---

### **Travel Tips for Non-EU Residents**

- Apply for your visa **at least 8–10 weeks** before DjangoCon Europe.
- Double-check the validity of your passport and supporting documents.
- Plan your travel and accommodation early: February is high ski season in Tyrol and Innsbruck fills up.

With proper preparation, securing your visa will be a smooth process. We look forward to seeing you at DjangoCon Europe in **Innsbruck, Austria**! 🎉
